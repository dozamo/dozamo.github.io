This fundamental domain covers identity and access management, the cornerstone of securing any multi-user Linux system. You will master the complete lifecycle of local user and group accounts, from their creation and modification to their eventual removal, ensuring proper ownership and permissions are maintained across the system.

Beyond simple account creation, this section explores how to customize the user experience and enforce system policy through personal and system-wide environment profiles. You will learn to control system resources by configuring resource limits (ulimit) for users, preventing a single user from destabilizing the entire server.

The domain then delves into advanced, fine-grained permission control with Access Control Lists (ACLs), allowing you to grant specific permissions to users and groups beyond the traditional owner/group/other model. Finally, it bridges the gap to enterprise-level identity management by covering how to configure the system to authenticate against a centralized LDAP directory, enabling seamless integration into a larger corporate infrastructure.
